# Jam & Bay Furniture

Build me a beautiful, high-converting website for a furniture company located in Abuja, Nigeria. Make sure it has a hero section with a couple of benefits of working with us (e.g. quality craftsmanship, durable materials, custom designs), a services section, a furniture listing section displaying the types of furniture available for sale (e.g. sofas, beds, wardrobes, tables, chairs) with pictures, prices, materials, dimensions, and other details, a why us section, reviews section, FAQ section, a last CTA section (e.g. call us, chat on WhatsApp, request a quote), and a contact section.

The website should not have customer sign-up or login. It should be connected to Supabase to store and retrieve furniture listings and customer reviews. The business owner should be able to add new furniture products and customer reviews without editing the website code. People should be able to search for furniture and filter by category and price.
